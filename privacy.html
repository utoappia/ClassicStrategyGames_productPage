Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

The short version: Classic Strategy Games collects no data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no servers of ours. Everything — the games, the AI opponents, your settings and your match history — runs and stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Settings (such as player colours and gameplay preferences) are stored locally on your device.
  • Match history and replays are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere by the app.
  • The AI opponents run entirely on your device. No game data is sent off the device to compute moves.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Purchase processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy or Google's privacy policy respectively.

Shared replay links

If you choose to share a replay link, the link itself contains that match's data — the game, the moves played, and the player display names shown in the app. Anyone you send the link to can view that replay. Sharing is always your action; the app never shares anything on its own.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
